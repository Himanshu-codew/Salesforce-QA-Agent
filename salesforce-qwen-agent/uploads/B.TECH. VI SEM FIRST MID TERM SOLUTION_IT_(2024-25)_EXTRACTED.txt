Q, 5 Discuss Sampling and Quantization in digital images.
Solution; : In order to become suitable for digital processing, an image function f(x.y) must be digitized both
spatially and in amplitude. Typically, a frame grabber or digitizer is used to sample and quantize the analogue
video signal. Hence in order to create an image which is digital, we need to covert continuous data into digital
fem. There are two steps in which it is done: Sampling and Quantization
The sampling rate determines the spatial resolution of the digitized image, while the quantization level determines the
number of grey levels in the digitized image. A magnitude of the sampled image is expressed as a digital value in image
processing. The transition between continuous values of the image function and its digital equivalent is called
quantization
